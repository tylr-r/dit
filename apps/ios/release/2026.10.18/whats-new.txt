New ways to practice Morse by ear:

- Listen to common words and Q codes, choose what you heard, and learn what each Q code means.
- Practice a CW conversation with an AI operator. Hear the other station, then key your reply, including continuous prosigns.
- Listen to your own text, pause or replay it, and optionally check your copy.

Also in this update:

- Revised onboarding with animated listening and recognition illustrations.
- Progress groups letters by mastery and shows how quickly you recognize them.
- Reveal a Practice hint for one attempt without affecting your score.
- Test your playback speed and pitch from Settings.
- Improved Morse timing, answer feedback, and mode selection.

Requires iOS 16.4 or later.
